Automated Testing & Certification Pipeline

Google Forms, Google Sheets, Zapier

A self-grading test system: a candidate takes a test through a Google Form, their answers are scored and logged to a Google Sheet, and a Zap watches that sheet, verifies the result, and sends the certificate to the examiner automatically.

Once it's set up, no one has to check a result or send an email by hand — the whole path from "test submitted" to result delivered runs on its own, branching automatically depending on the outcome.
